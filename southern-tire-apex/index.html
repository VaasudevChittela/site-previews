FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need an appointment?

Call ahead at (919) 362-8616 and we’ll get you on the schedule. Regular maintenance is easy to book, and we fit people in whenever we can.

Do you have my tire size in stock?

Usually, yes. If not, we can often have it here within a couple of hours. Use the tire size decoder above, then call us with your size.

Can you inspect a used car before I buy it?

Yes. Pre-purchase inspections are one of the things customers mention most. Call to set up a time.

Do you do NC state inspections?

Yes, we do North Carolina safety and emissions inspections.

Are you open on weekends?

We’re open Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 5:30 PM, and closed Saturday and Sunday. Holiday hours may differ.
