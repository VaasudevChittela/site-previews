FAQ

Good
questions

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but we can get busy, so calling ahead at (919) 303-0131 is the best way to get your car in quickly.

Can I drop off or pick up after hours?

Yes. Drop your car off after hours and pick it up after hours once it’s done, so you don’t have to take time off work.

How do I approve repairs?

We text you a clear estimate after we diagnose the problem. Nothing is done until you approve it.

How long does an NC inspection take?

With an appointment, inspections are often done in about 15 minutes.

What should I tell you when I call?

The year, make and model of your vehicle, plus what you’re noticing: sounds, warning lights, or how it’s driving.
