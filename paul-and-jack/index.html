FAQ

Before you visit

What time do you open?

All three cafés open at 7 AM every day. We close at 6 PM Monday through Thursday and 8 PM Friday through Sunday. Holiday hours may differ.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Yes, we offer gluten-free choices alongside our pastries. Ask the team what’s in the case today.

Can I order a whole cake?

Most of the cakes we sell by the slice are also available whole for birthdays and special occasions. Call your nearest café to arrange it.

Do you do pastries for events?

We regularly put together pastry spreads for meetings, parties and community events. Call ahead with your date and headcount.

Is there seating?

Every café offers dine-in and takeout. Seating can fill up at busy times, especially weekend mornings.
