Preguntas

Questions? We’ve got answers.

Can I order a whole tres leches cake?

Yes. Whole cakes are often in the case, and you can call (919) 469-0456 to order ahead for birthdays, weddings and parties.

Can you write a name or message on a cake?

Ask when you call or visit. We can add a message to a fresh cake so it’s ready to celebrate.

Do you do wedding cakes?

Couples have ordered our tres leches for their weddings. Call to talk about size, style and timing.

What are your hours?

8 AM to 9 PM, every day of the week. Holiday hours may differ.

Do you offer takeout or delivery?

Takeout, of course, and delivery is available too. Call for details.

Something sweet is waiting.
