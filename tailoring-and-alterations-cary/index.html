FAQ

Good to know

How far ahead should I book for a wedding dress?

Ideally six to eight weeks before your date, so there’s room for a first fitting, the alterations and a final fitting. Short on time? Call anyway: many clients mention being squeezed in close to their date.

Do you alter lehengas and stitch saree blouses?

Yes. Lehenga tailoring and blouse stitching are among the most-mentioned services in our reviews, alongside bridal gowns and bridesmaid dresses.

What should I bring to my fitting?

The shoes and undergarments you plan to wear with the outfit, so hems and fit can be set exactly right.

Where are you located?

On Edgemore Ave in Cary, NC 27519. Call or text (919) 451-7555 to schedule your fitting and get directions.

What are your hours?

9 AM to 7 PM, seven days a week. Holiday hours may differ.
